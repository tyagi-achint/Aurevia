Returns & Replacement

Customized and personalized products are non-returnable and non-refundable.

Replacements

Replacement requests are accepted only if:

  • Product received is damaged.
  • Product received is incorrect.
  • Manufacturing defect is identified.

Claim Period

Claims must be submitted within 48 hours of delivery with supporting photographs.

Approved Replacement

Once approved, Aurevia will replace the affected items or provide an appropriate resolution.

Non-Eligible Cases

  • Delays caused by courier partners

FAQ

Do you offer customization?

Yes. We offer logo branding, employee name personalization, custom packaging, and personalized message cards.

What is the minimum order quantity?

MOQ varies by product. Most corporate gift solutions start from 25 pieces.

Can I customize the gift combinations?

Absolutely. We can curate bespoke gift hampers based on your budget and occasion.

How long does production take?

Production timelines typically range from 7-21 working days depending on quantity and customization requirements.

Do you deliver across India?

Yes, we provide PAN India delivery.

Can you ship gifts directly to employees?

Yes, we can arrange individual deliveries to employee locations upon request.

Do you provide GST invoices?

Yes. All orders are billed with GST-compliant invoices.

Can I request a sample?

Yes. Samples are available for most products subject to availability. Sample charges and shipping costs are payable by the client. In certain cases, sample costs may be adjusted against a confirmed bulk order at Aurevia's discretion.

Sample Policy

  • Samples are chargeable.
  • Shipping charges are additional.
  • Customized samples may have separate development costs.
  • Sample charges are generally non-refundable unless specifically agreed upon in writing.
  • Sample availability may vary by product.
